# Research Your Idea with AI

Prices and limits last checked: November 2026. They change often, so confirm on each tool's site.

Free AI tools can draft in an hour what used to take a week of desk research. They also make mistakes, so treat their output as a first draft and check the facts yourself.

---

## Pick the right tool

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
|---|---|---|
| [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) Deep Research | Long reports on competitors and markets | Included. Limits refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap. You must be 18+. Exports reports to Google Docs. |
| [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) | A second deep research report; everyday questions | Limited deep research reports. Shows ads. |
| [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) | Quick answers with sources | 3 Pro Searches a day and 1 Research query a month |
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Writing, Lean Canvas, interview scripts; makes Word and PowerPoint files | Web search included; the Research mode is paid only |
| [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com) | Answering questions from your own notes, reports and PDFs | 100 notebooks with up to 50 sources each. Does not train on your uploads unless you send feedback. |

Free deep research runs are limited, so don't spend them on test prompts. If one tool is busy or out of runs, use another.

## Research tasks

| Task | Use | How |
|---|---|---|
| Competitive analysis | Gemini Deep Research | Run one report, then check each competitor's website and pricing yourself. Read real complaints on [G2](https://www.g2.com), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) and [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com). |
| Lean Canvas | Claude or ChatGPT | Have the AI draft all 9 boxes, labelling each point FACT or ASSUMPTION. Finish it on a one-page template such as [Miro's Lean Canvas](https://miro.com/templates/lean-canvas/) (free) or [LEANSpark](https://leanspark.ai/leancanvas) (free for one idea). |
| Customer personas | Claude or ChatGPT | Give it real quotes from reviews, Reddit or interviews. Treat personas as guesses until 5 or more real people confirm them. |
| Market size (TAM, SAM, SOM) | Claude or ChatGPT | Work bottom-up: number of customers x price per year. Take the counts from Statistics Canada, not from the AI's memory. |
| Customer interviews | Claude or ChatGPT | Write a "Mom Test" interview script, find people with a [Tally](https://tally.so) or [Google Forms](https://forms.google.com) screener, then load your notes into Gemini Notebook and ask for patterns with quotes. |
| Pricing | Perplexity or Gemini | Collect competitor prices (check every link), then run a short price survey. |
| Pitch deck | [Gamma](https://gamma.app) | Have the AI write a 10-slide outline from your Lean Canvas, paste it into Gamma, then edit by hand. |

The [prompt pack](prompts.md) has copy-paste prompts for these tasks.

## Canadian data sources

- **[Statistics Canada](https://www.statcan.gc.ca):** business counts by industry and province (for selling to businesses), and census data on population and income (for selling to consumers).
- **[ISED Financial Performance Data](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/financial-performance-data):** average revenue, costs and profit for small businesses in more than 1,000 industries.
- **[Innovation Canada Business Benefits Finder](https://innovation.canada.ca/innovation/s/group-groupe?language=en_CA):** grants, loans and advice that match your business.
- **[Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/):** how search interest changes over time and by province. It shows interest, not sales.
- **[Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com) and [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com):** a few free lookups for rough competitor website traffic and funding. Some funding details are paid only.

For free help from people, the [Ontario Small Business Advisory Centres](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-small-business-advisory-centre-locations) offer consultations and business plan reviews. Bring your AI-made canvas and deck.

## Check before you trust

AI tools often get facts wrong, and deep research reports are no exception. A 2025 Tow Center (Columbia University) study found AI search tools gave wrong answers to more than 60% of queries.

1. **Click every link.** If it's broken or goes to the wrong page, treat the fact as false.
2. **Find the number on the page yourself** (Ctrl+F). If you can't find it, don't use it.
3. **Use primary sources for key numbers:** Statistics Canada for market size, the competitor's own pricing page for prices.
4. **Ask a second AI the same question.** Where they disagree, dig in.

A useful follow-up prompt: "For each number in your answer, quote the exact sentence from the source and give the link. If you can't, mark it UNVERIFIED."

Red flags: very round numbers, "industry reports say", forecasts with no named publisher, and prices without a link.

## Keep your idea private

Most free AI tools can use your chats to train their AI unless you turn it off. Do this before you type in your idea.

| Tool | How to turn off training |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Settings > Data controls > "Improve the model for everyone" |
| Gemini | Settings & help > Activity > turn off "Keep Activity" (when it's on, human reviewers may read some chats) |
| Claude | claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls > "Help improve our AI models" (or use an incognito chat) |
| Perplexity | Account settings > Preferences > "AI Data Retention" |
| Gemini Notebook | Does not train on your uploads unless you send feedback |

- **Turning training off can't undo past training.** Turn it off before your first chat.
- **Skip the thumbs up and down buttons.** They can send the whole chat to the company for review or training, even with training turned off.
- **Describe your idea in general terms.** Keep the secret details (formula, code, partner names, customer lists) out of free tools.
- **Avoid China-based assistants** such as DeepSeek, Kimi and Qwen for confidential ideas.
