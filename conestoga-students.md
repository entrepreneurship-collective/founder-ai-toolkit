# Conestoga Student Resources

Offers last checked: November 2026. They change often, so confirm on each offer's page.

For Conestoga College students with a valid @conestogac.on.ca email who are researching a startup idea or building an MVP.

---

## Claim these first

Some approvals take days, so start early. Have your Conestoga login and a photo of your student ID (or proof of enrollment) ready.

1. **Test your Conestoga login.** Sign in to [Microsoft Copilot Chat](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/) and look for the "Protected" tag. Open IBISWorld or Statista through the library from home.
2. **Apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack).** Add and verify your Conestoga email on GitHub first, then upload proof of enrollment. Approval can take several days.
3. **Sign up for [Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students)** with your Conestoga email. No credit card needed.
4. **Claim [OpenAI's Codex credits](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001147-codex-for-university-students)** through SheerID, on the ChatGPT account you plan to keep.
5. **Claim the [Google AI Plus student offer](https://gemini.google/ca/students/?hl=en):** one free year for college students in Canada. It needs a card, so set a reminder to cancel before it renews, and check the offer's end date.
6. **Claim [Figma Education](https://www.figma.com/education/) and [Notion Education](https://www.notion.com/product/notion-for-education)** with your school email.
7. **Founders: apply to [Venture Lab](https://research.conestogac.on.ca/conestoga-entrepreneurship-collective/programs/venturelab).** Check the current application deadline.

## What Conestoga provides

- **Microsoft Copilot Chat (protected):** AI chat with data protection. Your chats aren't used to train AI models.
- **Microsoft 365 apps:** Word, Excel and PowerPoint on up to 5 devices while your Conestoga email is active.
- **[LinkedIn Learning](https://lib.conestogac.on.ca/lynda):** thousands of video courses.
- **Market research databases:** IBISWorld (industry reports), Statista (statistics), Business Source Complete, ABI/INFORM, CBCA Business, Nexis Uni and a directory of Canadian businesses. Start with the library's [Researching for Your Business Plan](https://lib.conestogac.on.ca/researching-business-plan) guide.
- **Other software:** Figma, Tableau, Power BI, QuickBooks Online and more on the [IT downloads page](https://it.conestogac.on.ca/downloads). Adobe Express and Creative Cloud are available to full-time students in eligible programs.

Check with your instructor before using AI for graded work.

## Computers

You don't need a powerful computer: the browser tools in this toolkit run on any recent laptop.

- **Borrow one:** the library [lends laptops](https://lib.conestogac.on.ca/Tech-Loans-Help/loans) for short periods.
- **Conestoga partner pricing:** IT Services lists student deals from Dell and Lenovo on its [offers page](https://it.conestogac.on.ca/support/offers).
- **Education stores:** [Apple](https://www.apple.com/ca-edu/store), [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/store/b/student) (Surface), [Dell](https://www.dell.com/en-ca/lp/students) and [Lenovo](https://www.lenovo.com/ca/en/student/) sell to Canadian college students at a discount, usually after you verify with your school email or student ID. Compare with regular sales before you buy.

## Startup programs

- **[Conestoga Entrepreneurship Collective](https://research.conestogac.on.ca/conestoga-entrepreneurship-collective):** open to Conestoga students and alumni. Venture Lab (any industry) and [Venture Lab for Tech](https://research.conestogac.on.ca/conestoga-entrepreneurship-collective/programs/venture-lab-for-tech) give you a coach and mentorship. Venture Lab for Tech adds access to Conestoga research centres and Communitech. Labs run 15 weeks, and intro workshops help you prepare. Co-op students can also spend a work term building their own startup through [Co-op in the Collective](https://research.conestogac.on.ca/conestoga-entrepreneurship-collective/programs/co-op-in-the-collective). Contact: entrepreneurship@conestogac.on.ca.
- **[Waterloo Region Small Business Centre](https://www.waterlooregionsmallbusiness.com/):** free advisor sessions and a weekly Startup Business Basics session if you live in the region. It also runs:
  - [Summer Company](https://www.waterlooregionsmallbusiness.com/starting/programs-sab/summer-company/): up to $3,000 plus mentoring for ages 15 to 29 returning to school.
  - [Starter Company Plus](https://www.waterlooregionsmallbusiness.com/growing/programs-gyb/starter-company-plus/): up to $5,000. Not open to full-time students, so it fits after graduation.
  - [Early Sparks](https://www.waterlooregionsmallbusiness.com/growing/programs-gyb/early-sparks/): a 6-week idea validation program for Waterloo Region residents.
- **[Communitech](https://communitech.ca/):** founder membership for Ontario tech product companies. Some events are open to students.
- **[Accelerator Centre](https://www.acceleratorcentre.com/ac-incubate/):** coaching, investor prep and partner credits for tech startups. Reduced fee, no equity, and you must incorporate.
- **[Futurpreneur](https://www.futurpreneur.ca/en/get-started/):** startup loans with no equity, plus mentoring, for founders aged 18 to 39 anywhere in Canada.

## Watch-outs

- Trials that need a card renew at full price. Cancel in time.
- Some educational licences, such as JetBrains, are not for commercial products. Check the terms before you use one for paid work.
- Keep personal and confidential data out of AI chats, even protected ones.
